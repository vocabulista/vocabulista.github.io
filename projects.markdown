<p class="page-intro">A selection of projects I've contributed to over the years.</p>

<div class="project">
  <h3 class="project-title">
    <a href="https://lacos.uni-koeln.de">LAC</a>
    <span class="project-tagline">— Language Archive Cologne</span>
  </h3>
  <p>A digital repository for language documentation. The archive holds primary language data, including audio and video recordings, transcriptions, translations, and annotations. Dedicated to the long-term preservation and accessibility of these resources for research, education, and cultural heritage.</p>
</div>

<div class="project">
  <h3 class="project-title">
    <a href="http://mpcorpus.org">MPCD</a>
    <span class="project-tagline">— Middle Persian Corpus and Dictionary</span>
  </h3>
  <p>A digital corpus and dictionary for Zoroastrian Middle Persian, powered by Treeflow, an online tool for creating digital dictionaries, managing texts, and annotating them. While it supports generic annotations, it is primarily tailored for working with and annotating Universal Dependencies (UD).</p>
</div>

<div class="project">
  <h3 class="project-title">
    <a href="https://vedaweb.uni-koeln.de">VedaWeb</a>
    <span class="project-tagline">— A Platform for Linguistic Research on Old Indic Texts</span>
  </h3>
  <p>A web-based, open-access platform for linguistic research on Old Indic texts. The pilot corpus is the Rigveda, made available in a digitally accessible, morphologically and metrically annotated form and searchable by lexicographic and corpus-linguistic criteria.</p>
</div>

<div class="project">
  <h3 class="project-title">
    <a href="http://kosh.uni-koeln.de">Kosh</a>
    <span class="project-tagline">— Turning XML Dictionaries into Searchable APIs</span>
  </h3>
  <p>Kosh simplifies the setup of XML-formatted dictionaries, exposing them as REST and GraphQL APIs with fine-grained full-text search.</p>
</div>

<div class="project">
  <h3 class="project-title">
    <a href="https://github.com/cceh/eisp">EISP</a>
    <span class="project-tagline">— Extract, Index and Search PDFs</span>
  </h3>
  <p>A tool to extract, index, and search any PDF containing text.</p>
</div>
