<p class="page-intro">A selection of projects I've contributed to over the years:</p>

<article class="project">
  <a class="project-link" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://lacos.uni-koeln.de">
    <span class="project-tag">LAC</span>
    <h3 class="project-name">Language Archive Cologne</h3>
  </a>
  <p class="project-desc">A digital repository for language documentation. The archive holds primary language data, including audio and video recordings, transcriptions, translations, and annotations. Dedicated to the long-term preservation and accessibility of these resources for research, education, and cultural heritage.</p>
</article>

<article class="project">
  <a class="project-link" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="http://mpcorpus.org">
    <span class="project-tag">MPCD</span>
    <h3 class="project-name">Middle Persian Corpus and Dictionary</h3>
  </a>
  <p class="project-desc">A digital corpus and dictionary for Zoroastrian Middle Persian, powered by Treeflow, an online tool for creating digital dictionaries, managing texts, and annotating them. Designed primarily for annotating Universal Dependencies (UD), with general annotation support as well.</p>
</article>

<article class="project">
  <a class="project-link" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://vedaweb.uni-koeln.de">
    <span class="project-tag">VedaWeb</span>
    <h3 class="project-name">A Platform for Linguistic Research on Old Indo-Aryan Texts</h3>
  </a>
  <p class="project-desc">A web-based, open-access platform for linguistic research on Old Indo-Aryan texts. The pilot corpus is the Rigveda, made available in a digitally accessible, morphologically and metrically annotated form and searchable by lexicographic and corpus-linguistic criteria.</p>
</article>

<article class="project">
  <a class="project-link" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="http://kosh.uni-koeln.de">
    <span class="project-tag">Kosh</span>
    <h3 class="project-name">Turning XML Dictionaries into Searchable APIs</h3>
  </a>
  <p class="project-desc">Kosh simplifies the setup of XML-formatted dictionaries, exposing them as REST and GraphQL APIs with fine-grained full-text search.</p>
</article>

<article class="project">
  <a class="project-link" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" href="https://www.linguee.es/">
    <span class="project-tag">Linguee</span>
    <h3 class="project-name">Dictionary and Translation Search Engine</h3>
  </a>
  <p class="project-desc">An online dictionary and translation search engine. During its first three years, I led the Spanish department, ensuring translation and corpus quality. I also oversaw editorial direction, public relations, and advisory work.</p>
</article>
